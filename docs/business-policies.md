# Business policies and implementation decisions

The BRD describes the intended workflows but leaves several operational edge cases open. These are the explicit v1 decisions implemented in Haven.

1. **One hotel and one room per reservation.** Guest count is a single integer. Capacity does not distinguish adults and children. Currency is SAR.
2. **Hotel-local clock.** `HOTEL_TIMEZONE` defaults to Asia/Riyadh. Arrival dates, no-show eligibility, and check-in time use this timezone. Event timestamps are stored in UTC. The frontend refreshes hotel time every 30 seconds; the API remains authoritative.
3. **Stay boundaries.** Checkout must follow arrival. Stays are limited to 365 nights. New reservations cannot start in the past. Same-day departures and arrivals do not overlap.
4. **Availability.** Active, in-service rooms form inventory. Confirmed and Checked In reservations consume one room per occupied night. Availability uses the maximum simultaneous consumption across the requested stay. Dirty or clean rooms still count toward future inventory; readiness is enforced at check-in.
5. **Out-of-order rooms.** They are excluded for every future date until restored. There is no scheduled maintenance range. Taking a room out of service is refused if it is occupied, has any active physical assignment, or would reduce inventory below existing commitments. Reception must first reassign or cancel affected bookings. Deactivating an in-service room uses the same guard.
6. **Pricing.** Booking stores a decimal nightly-rate snapshot and total. Admin price edits do not change existing bookings. An explicit reservation modification applies the current rate, which is displayed in the confirmation before saving.
7. **Guest modifications/cancellation.** Only the owner can operate a confirmed reservation, and only before its scheduled check-in instant. Reception and admin may cancel any still-confirmed reservation; modifications still require valid present/future arrival dates. Invalid modifications roll back without changing the original.
8. **Assignment after editing.** Date or room-type changes clear the physical assignment. Guest-count-only changes keep it. Physical rooms keep their room type after creation so historical assignments remain coherent.
9. **Physical assignment.** Rooms must match the booked type, be active and in service, and have no active overlapping assignment. Future reservations may be assigned a dirty or presently occupied room when the dates do not conflict. If preassignments fragment otherwise sufficient type-level inventory, reception must rearrange eligible future assignments before arrival.
10. **Check-in.** Only on the scheduled arrival date, at or after standard check-in time. No early or previous-day arrival handling. The physical room must be active, Vacant, Inspected, and In Service. No room transfer after check-in.
11. **Check-out.** Reception can record the actual departure of a checked-in guest, including one who leaves early or was not processed by the scheduled checkout time. This never extends the booking, charges for lateness, or authorizes a late checkout product. Reservation completion, dirty/vacant room state, and task creation commit together.
12. **Manual no-show.** Reception/admin may mark a confirmed reservation no-show at or after the scheduled check-in instant. No automatic job or extra grace period is configured. History and timestamps remain intact.
13. **Walk-ins.** The booking, assignment, and immediate check-in occur in one transaction. A failure leaves no partial reservation. New guest profiles created beforehand remain legitimate directory records if their booking fails. All normal date, capacity, price, availability, and readiness checks apply.
14. **Housekeeping assignment.** Checkout creates an unassigned Open task. Reception/admin assign an active housekeeper and priority. A same-day confirmed reservation already assigned to the room produces High priority. Only the assigned housekeeper may start and complete it. In-progress tasks must be completed before disabling that staff account; open tasks may be reassigned.
15. **Inspection.** Only reception/admin may inspect an active, vacant, Clean room in service. A completed cleaning task remains in history after inspection.
16. **Room issues.** Housekeepers can report issues only for rooms represented in their assigned task history. They see their own reports; reception/admin see all reports and resolve them. Reports and resolutions do not automatically change the room’s operational status.
17. **Guest profiles.** Registration creates a login-linked profile. Reception-created profiles can have optional email/phone and no login. Profiles are not automatically linked or merged by matching email, preventing accidental account takeover or history reassignment.
18. **Staff accounts.** Admin creates receptionist/housekeeping accounts and edits name/active status. The UI cannot create or modify another admin, change roles, or reset passwords. Initial admin creation is a local administrative CLI operation.
19. **History.** No destructive delete endpoints. Reservations remain in their terminal states. Rooms/types can be deactivated, with commitments protected. Inactive types cannot be newly booked but existing valid stays can finish.
20. **Concurrency.** The hotel-row lock is held until the request transaction commits or rolls back. All inventory-affecting routes use it. PostgreSQL additionally rejects physically overlapping active room assignments. SQLite tests do not establish concurrency guarantees.

These policies stay within the BRD’s intended single-hotel operating model and can be adjusted explicitly if the hotel chooses different rules.
